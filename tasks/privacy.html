Tasks – Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Tasks is a to-do and reminder app for Android. It has no servers and no accounts of its own. The developer cannot see your tasks.

Data on your phone

Your tasks, categories and settings are stored only on your phone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks for access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It saves one backup file of your tasks there, about once a night, and reads it back only when you choose to restore. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive. The backup goes straight from your phone to your Google account; it is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

You can turn the backup off at any time in Settings. To delete the backup, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Tasks and choose "Delete hidden app data".

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Phone calendar (optional)

If you turn on calendar sync, tasks are copied into a calendar on your phone. This stays on your phone and in the calendar account you choose.

Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions. This request does not include any of your tasks.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or send your data to anyone. It has no ads and no analytics.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
